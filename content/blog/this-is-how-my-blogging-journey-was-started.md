I wrote my first blog on **[Medium](https://s4gor.medium.com/wc-get-orders-function-from-woocommerce-a-leading-wordpress-e-commerce-plugin-51e95b241c36 "Medium")** on Nov 24, 2023. I wrote the first blog which can be found on this website also named "[**wc_get_orders() function from WooCommerce, A WordPress E-commerce plugin**](../blog/wc-get-orders-function-from-woocommerce-a-wordpress-e-commerce-plugin)". That was about a special function provided by **[Woocommerce](https://woocommerce.com/ "WooCommerce")** that retrieves order data from the database. I was researching this one because I planned to build an analytical dashboard for websites powered by **[Woocommerce](https://woocommerce.com/ "WooCommerce")** where the owner can see helpful analytics of their orders.

![](https://old.jstales.com/storage/uploads/Screenshot 2025-01-22 035910.png)

My first post on medium

I came to understand that **[Woocommerce](https://woocommerce.com/ "WooCommerce")** provides this kind of facility by default. So I checked [**Woocommerce**'s](https://woocommerce.com/ "WooCommerce") default dashboard and didn't find anything helpful. All I needed was an advanced analytics dashboard that benefits users. But that didn't work out the way I thought. I ended up writing about the knowledge I learned from researching **[Woocommerce](https://woocommerce.com/ "WooCommerce")**'s plugin. I forgot about the blog and was devoted to keeping my academic legacy by passing the final examination for my BBA degree. I have to say that I came up with lots of ideas which weren't successful at all. But I am a man in a can! All I can do is try again again and again. Some months ago, I created a **Chrome Extension Developer** **account** to release my simple ideas on the tab for **Chrome** users. I am using a very popular chrome extension named **"[Project Naptha](https://antimatter15.com/project/project-naptha/ "Project Naptha")"** developed by **Kevin Kwok** aka "**Antimatter15**". He shared lots of his ideas and projects on his **[website](https://antimatter15.com "Antimatter15")**. I got inspiration from him. And frankly speaking, my full website is motivated by his website. I have to admire him—a pure genius guy.

I am a freelance web developer. I am an expert at building websites with **Shopify** and **WordPress**. I have been working as a freelance **Shopify** and **WordPress** developer for five years. So it was easy for me to build a blogging website using **WordPress** but I prefer a custom site where I can experience real-life problems. So I used **Laravel**, a **PHP** framework, to build this blogging site. I know I can create it in the blink of an eye with **WordPress** but I wanted to learn how things related to programming work. It took like 7 days to complete this site with basic CRUD features. 

while building this site, I faced some critical processes like integrating **[TinyMCE](https://www.tiny.cloud/)**, and currently using it as my blog editor, free version though. One thing that stressed me was **the Elequent Model** of Laravel. Like you can't use it. You can't have this function for this query builders, blah blah blah!!!!! But the site is ready now and I am happy for it. I know anyone may wonder why I need to make this extra effort when I can use **Medium**, **Stackoverflow Blog**, **Quora**, etc! I wanted something like **Kevin Kwok**'s website and the interesting thing is he wrote his last project/blog, whatever you say, in 2015. And now 2024 is ending. Someone is motivated by **Kevin** 9 years later. I believe one day someone will find my ordinary ideas that may motivate him/her! This may not make any big difference, but that's my small happiness.
